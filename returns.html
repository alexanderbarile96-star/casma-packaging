Return Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Our Commitment

At Casma Beekeeping and Packaging, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If something isn't right, please contact us as soon as possible and we'll work to resolve it promptly.

Damaged or Incorrect Goods

If you receive goods that are damaged in transit or incorrect, please:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order
  • Email sales@casmapack.com.au with your order details and photos of the damage or incorrect items
  • Retain all original packaging until the matter is resolved

We will arrange a replacement, credit, or refund at our discretion depending on the nature of the issue.

Change of Mind Returns

We do not accept change of mind returns. Please ensure you select the correct product, size, and quantity before placing your order. If you're unsure, contact us before ordering and we'll be happy to help.

Faulty Products

If a product is found to be faulty (not caused by misuse or improper storage), please contact us within 7 days of receipt. We will assess the claim and offer a replacement or credit where appropriate.

How to Lodge a Return

  1. Email sales@casmapack.com.au with your name, order number, and description of the issue
  2. Attach photos clearly showing the issue
  3. Our team will respond within one business day with next steps
  4. Do not return goods without receiving a Return Authorisation from us first

Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days via the original payment method (bank transfer or card refund). Freight costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Contact Us

For any return enquiries, please reach out:

  • Email: sales@casmapack.com.au
  • Phone: 0415 481 375
  • Hours: Monday–Friday 9am–5pm, Saturday 9am–12pm
