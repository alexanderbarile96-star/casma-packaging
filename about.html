About Casma

Who We Are

Your one-stop packaging warehouse in WA

Casma Packaging commenced operations in August 2020 — right in the thick of the COVID-19 pandemic. Starting as a lean Western Australian enterprise focused on supplying glass jars for food packaging, we proved that quality and service could thrive even in uncertain times.

In 2022, we expanded into beekeeping equipment, recognising the strong demand from WA's growing apiary community. By 2023, we officially rebranded as Casma Beekeeping and Packaging to reflect the breadth of what we now offer.

Today we stock glass food jars, plastic bottles, food-grade buckets and pails, metal closures, and beekeeping supplies — all from our warehouse in Wangara, Perth.
