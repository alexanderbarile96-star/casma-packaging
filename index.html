Our Story

WA's one-stop packaging warehouse

Casma Beekeeping & Packaging started in August 2020 right here in Western Australia. What began as a specialist glass jar supplier has grown into a comprehensive packaging and beekeeping supply business.

We're located in Wangara and stock everything from small honey jars to large food-grade pails — with no minimum order and generous bulk discounts.
